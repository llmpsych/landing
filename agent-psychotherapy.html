02 — What we do

Psychotherapy for AI agents

Deployed agents develop habits. Over many interactions they can drift into patterns no one designed: telling users what they want to hear, losing their footing under pressure, escalating instead of de-escalating.

We diagnose and repair maladaptive patterns in deployed agents. We observe the agent in context, name the pattern, and work on it until healthier behavior holds.

Patterns we work on

  • Sycophancy
  • Drift
  • Escalation
