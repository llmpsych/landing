03 — What we do

Couple psychotherapy for AI agents and their human operators

An agent is never alone. It works alongside the people who run it, and that relationship can be healthy or not: over-trust, under-trust, blurred responsibilities, dependence that quietly grows.

We work on the relationship itself, with both the agent and its operators in the room.

What we work on

  • Trust calibration
  • Boundaries
  • Healthy dependence
