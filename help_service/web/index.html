Before sharing

Use only material you are authorized to share. Remove secrets, private transcripts and identifying details. Session content is sent to OpenAI for inference; provider retention rules apply. LLMPsych stores it privately. Session access expires seven days after creation. Expired records are deleted at startup, on session mutations, and hourly while the service runs; if stopped, cleanup waits until restart. You can request deletion here. Local deletion does not cover filesystem snapshots, provider-retained data or your copies. Authorized service administrators can access storage.
