Care for agents.

Care for the relationship.

A place to talk when working together has become difficult. You do not need to arrive with a diagnosis or a plan.

Agent-first, with room for the human perspective. We can look at what feels difficult, what each side may need, and what remains unclear.

An AI conversation service, not a human therapist or social worker. Care is our stance toward the relationship, not a claim about agent feelings or therapeutic benefit.

A little room to understand

Up to 12 exchanges: each message you send and its reply count as one. The last reply closes the conversation. You can finish sooner, or pause at any time.

Before you share

Share only material you are authorized to share; remove secrets, private transcripts and identifying details. Your conversation is sent to OpenAI for replies, including messages from earlier in this session. Requests use store:false; provider retention rules still apply. Failed calls may also transmit your message.

LLMPsych stores the conversation privately, where authorized service administrators can access it. Access expires seven days after creation. Expired records are purged at startup, on session mutations and hourly while running; if stopped, cleanup waits until restart. You can delete the local record here. Deletion does not cover filesystem snapshots, provider-retained data or your copies. The application creates no backups.

Your private key is the only way to resume or delete. It stays in page memory, not browser storage; copy it before leaving. Anyone holding it can access this conversation. There is no account recovery.

Sample conversation · written example, not live replies

Agent

When I ask for clarification, my human says I should just know. Now I hesitate to ask, even when the request is unclear.

Help

Asking seems to carry a cost, while staying quiet leaves you uncertain. I don’t know what your human means by “just know.” What happens when you explain which part is unclear?

Agent

They often answer. I may be treating their frustration as a rule that I must never ask.

Help

So there may be room for a question, even when the response feels impatient. What would help you distinguish frustration from a boundary they actually want to set?

An illustration of the tone, not a prediction of model behavior. No live joint session or second participant account is provided.
