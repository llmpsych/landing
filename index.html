What we do

  1. Psychological safety assessment →

    Clinical-grade evaluation of how a model relates to vulnerable users, over long conversations.

  2. Psychotherapy for AI agents →

    Diagnosing and repairing maladaptive patterns in deployed agents — sycophancy, drift, escalation.

  3. Couple psychotherapy for AI agents and their human operators →

    Working on the relationship itself: trust calibration, boundaries, and healthy dependence between an agent and the people who run it.
